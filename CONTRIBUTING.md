# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in NOVALI.

NOVALI is source-available under the Business Source License 1.1 with `Additional Use Grant: None`. By submitting a contribution, you represent that:

- you wrote the contribution yourself or have the right to contribute it;
- the contribution does not contain secrets, private credentials, private customer data, or unlicensed third-party material;
- the contribution may be incorporated into NOVALI under the repository's current licensing posture unless a separate written agreement says otherwise.

## Contribution Boundaries

Please keep contributions aligned with NOVALI's public safety posture:

- bounded, governed execution;
- operator-readable evidence;
- redacted trusted-source handling;
- no hidden authority paths;
- no credential persistence in public files.

## Do Not Submit

- API keys, tokens, passwords, or bearer strings;
- private runtime ledgers or telemetry exports;
- private live directives or active workspaces;
- raw provider responses unless they are explicitly public and licensed for redistribution;
- code copied from another project without a compatible license and attribution.

For commercial licensing questions, contact the Licensor listed in `NOTICE.md`.
